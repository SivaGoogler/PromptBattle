# PromptBattle

## AI for Kids Video Project

This project contains the complete production assets for an educational video series about Artificial Intelligence for children, created as part of the "Prompt Battle 2026" competition.

### Project Overview

The video explores how AI can help children in their daily lives, from education to creativity, presented through a series of 8 cinematic scenes set in a Tamil village context.

### Structure

- `prompt/` - Contains detailed JSON scene descriptions for video generation
  - Each scene includes video prompts, voiceover scripts, audio cues, and production notes
  - Scenes are designed for 7-second durations, totaling approximately 56 seconds
- `video/` - Final rendered video output
  - `AI_KIDS_FINAL_VIDEO.mp4` - The complete assembled video

### Scene Breakdown

1. **The World Waking Up** - Introduction to AI in a traditional village setting
2. **AI Helping in the Classroom** - Educational assistance through interactive technology
3. **Creative AI Art** - AI-powered artistic expression
4. **AI Health Companion** - Medical and wellness support
5. **AI Language Learning** - Multilingual education and communication
6. **AI Environmental Awareness** - Technology for sustainability
7. **Future Dreams** - Aspirational view of AI's potential
8. **Unity in Technology** - Conclusion emphasizing inclusive AI benefits

### Production Tools

- **Video Generation**: Google Labs Veo 2
- **Text-to-Speech**: TTSMaker.com
- **Format**: 4K, 16:9 aspect ratio, photorealistic cinematic style

### Usage

Each scene JSON file contains all necessary prompts and specifications for recreating or modifying the video content. The detailed prompts include subject descriptions, camera movements, audio specifications, and creative enhancements.
